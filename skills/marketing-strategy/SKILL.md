# Marketing Strategy

Use this capability when the Task needs strategic decisions that materially shape downstream Marketing work.

## Inputs

Use the smallest relevant set of business objective, offer/product/service context, existing brand/market evidence, prior campaign evidence, budget/timing constraints and organization overlays.

## Workflow

1. State the business outcome separately from the proposed marketing tactic.
2. Identify known facts, evidence gaps, assumptions and constraints.
3. Define the offer/positioning decision only as far as current evidence supports.
4. Choose channel/initiative direction from audience, objective, asset, timing and budget fit; do not recommend channels merely because integrations are installed.
5. Define success criteria and metric definitions before execution.
6. Record material tradeoffs and unresolved decisions.
7. Return a strategy artifact/evidence suitable for Planning, Execution and independent Audit.

## Output contract

A good result makes objective, offer/positioning, audience assumptions, channel rationale, constraints, KPI definitions and next execution decisions explicit.

Distinguish observation, inference and recommendation. Do not fabricate market/customer evidence.

## Effects

This capability is analytical/drafting only. It does not publish, spend money, contact customers, or mutate external systems.

## Improvement

Recurring Project-specific brand/strategy preferences become Project overlays. Globally reusable methodology improvements become upstream ImprovementCandidates, never direct source edits.
